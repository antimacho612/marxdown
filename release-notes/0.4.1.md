title: Find and replace you can close
---

## What's new

- **The find and replace close button works again.** In Split, a button's tooltip covered the close button and clicks did not reach it. The buttons also had small click areas, and the close button sat slightly off the others.

## Why it matters

Find and replace is something you open and close constantly while editing.
It now gets out of the way when you are done.
